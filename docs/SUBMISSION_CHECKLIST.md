- [ ] Hosted URL opens without local setup.
- [ ] Demo begins with a visible data-status label.
- [ ] At least one station displays saved holdout metrics versus persistence.
- [ ] No random or synthetic result is called live, CPCB, OpenAQ, measured, or validated.

- [ ] Slides include problem, user, workflow, architecture, validation, impact, scale and roadmap.
- [ ] Video is 2–3 minutes and follows `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`.
- [ ] Submit the repository, deck, video and hosted URL.
